Carter Ryan Smith — born August 8, 2005 in Monroe, Michigan — is the American tech creator behind CarterPCs. At 18, he packed up and moved to Los Angeles to turn a phone-filmed TikTok hobby into a full-blown content machine.

He posts four times a day about PCs, phones, EVs, AI, and whatever tech is currently exciting (or genuinely ridiculous). No scripts that sound like scripts. No 12-minute intros. Just the gear, the takes, and the occasional hot take.
